# Ready-to-share project update

Tessarchy is moving from a VM prototype toward a real handheld device.

Quiet Edge brings glass navigation, a phone-style app switcher and adaptive
phone/tablet/desktop layouts. The new four-digit PIN login has been tested through
real greetd/PAM authentication in the development VM. Bluetooth, media, audio and
screenshot controls are now integrated in development source, with remaining
hardware and runtime validation tracked publicly.

The refreshed site has real VM screenshots, an official logo kit with a spinning
GIF, and a professional roadmap from prototype to a phone or tablet in hand.

Explore: https://thediamondsaint.github.io/tessarchy/
Source: https://github.com/thediamondsaint/tessarchy
Roadmap: https://thediamondsaint.github.io/tessarchy/assets/tessarchy-device-roadmap.png

The downloadable Alpha 2 ISO remains an experimental x86_64 VM tester image and
predates the latest development additions. Physical phone/tablet support is a
planned milestone, not a supported product claim yet.
